Hosting VET Students from Metsovo, Greece

As a project partner, we hosted an international mobility activity in İzmir as part of the Erasmus+ project "K121 VET Information Systems in Economics - Administration" (2025-1-EL01-KA122-VET-000329554), welcoming a group of vocational high school students from 1o EPAL METSOVOU in Metsovo, Greece. 16.03.2026Erasmus+ KA1 VET

Exploring Entrepreneurship at Bambu, DEÜ Teknopark

We had the opportunity to visit the Bambu Entrepreneurship Office at Dokuz Eylül University Technopark together with our Greek students. During this inspiring visit, students gained valuable insights into entrepreneurship, innovation, and company formation processes — a practical overview of how ideas evolve into startups, covering business development, incubation support, and the entrepreneurial ecosystem within the technopark.

It was a highly engaging experience where students explored real-world examples and learned about the steps required to establish and grow a company in a dynamic innovation environment. A big thank you to the Bambu team for hosting us and sharing their expertise!

14.03.2026Erasmus+ KA1 VET

Exploring Smart Tourism at Renaissance İzmir Hotel

As part of our mobility activities in İzmir, we had the pleasure of visiting the Renaissance İzmir Hotel, a member of the Marriott International group. Our students had the opportunity to closely observe how CRM (Customer Relationship Management) and ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) systems are used in real tourism operations — from managing guest experiences to optimizing internal processes.

This hands-on experience allowed our students to connect theory with practice and better understand the digital transformation of the tourism sector. We thank the Renaissance İzmir Hotel team for their warm welcome and for sharing their expertise with our students.

12.03.2026Erasmus+ KA1 VET

Visit to Yaşar University

As part of our Erasmus+ mobility, we had the opportunity to visit Yaşar University in İzmir. The International Relations Office welcomed us warmly and gave a heartfelt presentation about the university's international vision and the opportunities it offers students.

We also attended an inspiring session on software systems, CRM, and ERP, delivered by Assoc. Prof. Korhan Karabulut (Software Engineering Department), Research Assistant Hüseyin Ozan Altın, and Prof. Ahmet Tuncay Ercan (Management Information Systems Department), together with Dr. Efthymia Staiou from the Industrial Engineering Department. They provided valuable insights into how CRM and ERP systems are developed and applied in real business environments, as well as the fundamentals of modern software systems.

Dr. Staiou being Greek was a wonderful coincidence, creating a special connection with our Greek group — part of the session was even delivered in Greek, making the experience even more interactive and culturally engaging. We thank Yaşar University for their kind hospitality.
